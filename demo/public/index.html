Trip Research Synthesizer

Give it a destination, dates, and who's coming, and it turns real trip research — TripAdvisor, Google Reviews, Yelp, local tourism boards — into one reconciled, group-filtered shortlist. It flags what's a good fit for the youngest and oldest in your group, calls out duplicates or conflicts with any notes a friend already sent you, and pauses for your approval before adding weather, travel time, and cost estimates.

Live demo — real web search, run via the Anthropic API. This can take up to a minute per step.
