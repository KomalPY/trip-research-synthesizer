How it works

Two review points keep you in control before any effort goes into logistics.

  1. Tell it the tripDestination, dates, group size, who's traveling, and any expectations, like one medium hike or fall colors.
  2. Review the draftPlaces and activities only, grouped by area and loose day range. Say what to add, cut or swap.
  3. Approve to add logisticsWeather, travel time, transport and cost for each item, then a final round of feedback.

What it flags for you

A shortlist is only useful if you know which parts to double-check.

Verify before you plan around it

Closures, seasonal hours and event dates that could be stale are marked, with what to check.

Local and tourist picks, both

Every item is tagged, so you get the safe bets and the differentiated ones, not only the famous list.

Fit for the whole group

Each item says who it suits and offers an alternate when it doesn't suit everyone.
